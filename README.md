# master-builders-vr
The father of VR games - A pitch by Pedro Simental Jacinto - Open world VR MMO with free modeling crafting + AI Nomad NPCs


Subject: Project: Master Builders - The Father of VR Games - Pitch from a Player

Hi Indie Devs,

My name is Pedro Simental Jacinto from Guaymas, Sonora, Mexico. I'm not a dev, I'm a player, and I have the idea for the game that should be the father of all VR games.

Think "Everything is Awesome!" (The LEGO Movie) but as a real VR MMO.

Core Concept - No Recipes:
In VR you model items from scratch with your hands, part by part (blade, hilt, treated leather) like Enshrouded building, not realistic physics. Then you name it and tell the game what it is for with a command: Create as [SWORD / CHAIR / SADDLE GEAR].

The game, assisted by AI, understands what you built. It gives stats based on materials and efficiency. If you built a beautiful but useless sword, it doesn't delete it. It registers it as a Decoration Item and the game itself makes you an Interior Decorator. The game tells you who you are.

Base Branches - Herrero + Carpintero + Cocinero + Nomad:

Blacksmith, Carpenter, Cook, Nomad.
From Nomad you unlock: Warrior, Dungeon Looter, Gatherer, Gladiator, Soldier, Guardian, etc.
Level system: - Apprentice, Basic, + Expert, ++ Master, +++ Legendary. You need to pass through one branch to unlock the next. You can level 2 branches at the same time. Farming is slow on purpose. To reach max potential you need 5 years playing 24/7 and you only get 1/3 of it.
World & Economy - Like Medieval Dynasty:
No predefined houses. No predefined story. GW2-style big worlds with safe points where no damage. The economy is 100% player-driven like Medieval Dynasty. A well-organized village is the most powerful, but a solo Nomad +++ can be a super strong and self-sufficient savage living in a hut made of banana leaves. Because every item has predefined values - a big banana leaf can be provisional clothing, weapon wrap, or roof.

Autonomous AI NPCs:
Not standing NPCs. Real Nomad NPCs living in real-time on the server, exploring by themselves. You can stop them on the road and ask: "Have you seen black iron?" and they answer from their real memory: "Yes, 30 mins ago in the sulfur crack, but there were big red-eyed bugs."

Endgame - The 4 Sleepers:
Only 4 Legendary Bosses in the whole game, sleeping, hidden in different biomes, super hard to find. Temporary dungeons appear for 1 week max to be looted, then disappear. They don't give weapons, they give first-quality materials that don't exist elsewhere.

To find a Sleeper you must go deep in a dungeon. When you find it, you get trapped with it and it kills you. You respawn, but your loot stays there, saved on the server with that boss. You have 10 mins to come back, if not, your loot stays there until 50 high-level Warriors come and kill the boss. The boss becomes a chest full of stories from dead players.

Housing - Sacred 4x4 Hut:
Land is sold by the server, 1 per player, 1 account per Google / Meta Unlock email + IP. Size of a small hut (4x4). Only you can edit it. No offline raiding. Your hut is sacred. But you must visit it at least 1 time per month to keep it active, or it becomes a lootable ruin. This keeps the community alive.

It's possible today. Quest performance using PC RAM/SSD power. OpenXR.

This is the game I want to play.

Thanks for reading.

- Pedro Simental Jacinto
