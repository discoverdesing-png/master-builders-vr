# Master Builders VR - TEST BASE: The Nomad Refugee (MVP 0.1)

**Pitch by Pedro Simental Jacinto - Guaymas, Sonora, Mexico**

> The game doesn't start in a city. It starts on the road. You are not a hero. You are a refugee from a country destroyed by a Legendary Beast.

This is the realistic test base, not the full MMO. This is what an indie team can ship in 2 months.

---

## The Test Lore

You arrive from a neighboring country that was wiped out by one of the 4 Legendary Sleeping Beasts. You have nothing. You walk on a dirt path into new lands.

You are a Nomad.

### The Initial Map - At Your Feet

For this test, there is NO civilization. Only:

*   **Everything is touchable:** All vegetation can be touched, picked, cut
*   **Wild animals only:** Local fauna (boars, rabbits, wolves) - No monsters yet
*   **One short path:** A dirt road that goes to a small hill
*   **The Choice Hill:** On top of the hill, you choose your first direction. This is where the test ends and Phase 2 begins.

This is 1 small map: ~200 trees, 20 animals, 1 hill. Buildable by 1 person in Unity.

---

## Level Cap System - The Key to Balance

To prevent "max level in 1 day", the Test Map is hard-capped.

**Level System:**

*   `-` = Level -1: Knows nothing, Apprentice-
*   (no sign) = Level 2: Beginner
*   `+` = Level 3: Helper
*   `++` = Level 4: Official
*   `+++` = Level 5: Master

**For TEST BASE MVP, MAX CAP = Level 2 (Beginner Nomad)**

*   You start as: **Nomad -** (Level -1, you don't even know how to grab a branch)
*   You end as: **Nomad** (Level 2, plain Nomad)

You CANNOT go to + or beyond in this map. The game stops you.

### Inside Nomad, you can only peek at 3 branches, also capped at Level 2:

*   Nomad -> Blacksmith - (Apprentice Blacksmith-)
*   Nomad -> Blacksmith (Beginner Blacksmith)
*   Nomad -> Carpenter - 
*   Nomad -> Carpenter
*   Nomad -> Hunter -
*   Nomad -> Hunter

You cannot become Blacksmith +, ++, or +++. That is Phase 2 when the village exists.

---

## Why This Prevents OP Weapons (The Economy Base)

Because you are capped at Beginner, the strongest weapon you can make is as strong as the local animals.

Example:

*   Local Boar: 20 HP, 5 blunt
*   Best Test Sword: Pine Wood (5 dur) + Boar Soul x1 (+5 str) = 10-15 power. Can kill a boar, but barely.
*   You CANNOT make a 100 power sword because you don't have Black Iron and you don't have Soul slots.

This creates a **base economy**: Players trade weak but useful gear. No one is OP. Everyone is equally weak. Perfect for testing trading and crafting time.

---

## Crafting System - 3 Layers (Test Version)

**1. Material Layer (Fixed):** Banana Leaf = 0 edge, Pine Wood = 5 dur, etc.

**2. Form Layer (Validation):** 
*   Sword = Blade + Grip + Balance
*   If invalid -> Decoration (You are still a Decorator Nomad)

**3. Soul Layer (Personality by Accumulation):**

In Test Map, only Boar, Rabbit, Wolf Souls exist.

*   **1 Boar Soul:** +5 strength, weapon grunts
*   **3 Boar Souls (MAX in test):** +12 strength (diminishing), Personality: **Monster Attraction (Small)** - Small animals are attracted within 15m. Strong but risky.

Max 3 Souls in test to prevent stacking.

**Formula (Capped):** `Power = (Materials + Structure + Souls) - Capped to 20 max for Test Map`

## What We Test in This Base

1.  Does touching everything feel good in VR?
2.  Does building by hand feel magical?
3.  Does hearing "You are a Nomad Hunter -" feel like identity?
4.  Is 30 min crafting time for a real sword fun or tedious?
5.  Can players trade weak gear and create economy?
6.  Does the Choice Hill make you want Phase 2?

If YES to these 6, we have a game. If not, we fix the core loop before adding MMO systems.
